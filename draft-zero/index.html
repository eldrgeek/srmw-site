What was NaNoWriMo?

The original book's second chapter is called What Is NaNoWriMo? The answer was National Novel Writing Month: fifty thousand words, starting after midnight on November 1.

NaNoWriMo closed on March 31, 2025. That was six months after a public fight over its statement on AI in writing. The book's only link, nanowrimo.org, now forwards to an empty page. So the chapter has changed its tense.

The tradition carries on without it, most visibly in Novel November.

How the words count

NaNoWriMo ended arguing about whether words written with AI should count. Here they count separately and in the open. Every paragraph below says who wrote it, and every AI paragraph says which model wrote it.
